Murray Marshall · Team Ontario

The Story Behind #6

Baseball
runs in the family.

My dad Murray was the first inductee into the Team Ontario Baseball Hall of Fame. Associate scout for the Toronto Blue Jays. He started the Hamilton Astros. His #6 jersey is retired.

I grew up in dugouts. He taught me to coach, taught me to play, and taught me that a good glove is worth fixing. That's what this business is.
